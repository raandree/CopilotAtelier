# Explain it simpler

Re-explain the named subject, or the last answer when none is named, one familiarity level lower in each knowledge area involved: from `expert` to `familiar`, from `familiar` to `new`. Follow the `contributor-calibration` Instruction.

- Lead with what it means for the contributor and what they need to decide or do.
- Keep every fact, caveat, warning, and open question; simplify the language only.
- Keep each precise term, followed by its plain meaning.
- Already at `new`: use smaller steps and one everyday comparison, then ask which part is still unclear, offering two to four options.
- Re-ask each open question with its recommended answer and a `not sure, you pick` option.
- End with one line naming each knowledge area and the level it keeps for the rest of the session.
- When this session received a contributor-profile sentence and the area is declared there, offer once per area to keep the level: `Save Kerberos = new to your profile? (yes / no)`.
- Change no files.
