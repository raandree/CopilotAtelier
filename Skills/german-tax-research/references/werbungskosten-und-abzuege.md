## Grundsatz und Pauschbetrag

`§ 9 Abs. 1 S. 1 EStG`: expenses for acquiring, securing, and maintaining income. Private living costs are barred by `§ 12 Nr. 1 EStG`. Mixed expenses are apportionable where an objective key exists (BFH GrS 1/06); where none exists, the whole amount falls away.

The `Arbeitnehmer-Pauschbetrag` (`§ 9a S. 1 Nr. 1 a EStG`) is granted automatically, so itemised expenses only bite above it — check that threshold before assembling evidence for a small position. From VZ 2026, union dues are deducted **in addition** to the flat amount (`§ 9a S. 3 EStG`).

## Wege zur ersten Tätigkeitsstätte

`Entfernungspauschale`, `§ 9 Abs. 1 S. 3 Nr. 4 EStG`: per full kilometre of the shortest road distance, for each working day with one trip to the first place of work. Only one trip per day counts, regardless of how many were made. The rate per kilometre and its steps are year-dependent — see [`kennzahlen.md`](kennzahlen.md).

The ceiling of 4,500 euro applies unless the taxpayer used their own or a company car; public transport costs above the flat rate may be claimed at actual cost. A longer route counts where it is demonstrably more convenient and was regularly used.

`Erste Tätigkeitsstätte` (`§ 9 Abs. 4 EStG`) is defined by permanent assignment, in the employment contract or in fact. Without one, every trip is `Auswärtstätigkeit` at travel-cost rules, which are more favourable — check the assignment before applying the commuting rate.

## Auswärtstätigkeit und Reisekosten

Four cost blocks, each with its own evidence:

| Block | Rule | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Fahrtkosten | Actual cost, or 0.30 euro per kilometre driven for a car (`§ 9 Abs. 1 S. 3 Nr. 4a S. 2 EStG` with the BRKG rate) | Tickets, mileage log with date, route, purpose |
| Verpflegung | Statutory flat rates only, never actual cost (`§ 9 Abs. 4a EStG`) | Proof of duration and place of the trip |
| Übernachtung | Actual cost | Hotel invoice in the taxpayer's name |
| Reisenebenkosten | Actual cost | Receipts for parking, luggage, tolls, business calls |

Two rules that decide most disputes. **Overnight cost has no flat rate for employees**: without an invoice the amount is not deductible, and a domestic or foreign accommodation flat rate cannot be substituted. And meal allowances are **reduced** where meals were provided — 20 % of the full daily rate for breakfast, 40 % each for lunch and dinner (`§ 9 Abs. 4a S. 8 EStG`) — so a conference fee including catering cuts the allowance.

The allowance stops after three months at the same external place of work (`§ 9 Abs. 4a S. 6 EStG`), restarting after a four-week interruption. Foreign per-diems and accommodation rates follow the annual BMF letter; use the table for the year in question and cite it.

Where a receipt is genuinely unobtainable — a cash fare, a ticket machine without a printout — write a signed `Eigenbeleg` stating date, amount, purpose, payee, and why no receipt exists. It is weaker than an invoice but far better than an undocumented line. Template in [`vorlagen.md`](vorlagen.md).

## Fortbildung, Ausbildung, Tagungen

Deductible as `Werbungskosten` where occasioned by the current or a concretely intended occupation. Cost of a **first** vocational training or first degree outside an employment relationship is only a `Sonderausgabe` up to the annual cap (`§ 9 Abs. 6`, `§ 10 Abs. 1 Nr. 7 EStG`), and since it is a special expense it is lost in a year without sufficient income.

For each event, assemble the four proof types from the main Skill:

1. Fee invoice — or, where the event was free, an express statement that no fee is claimed.
2. Payment evidence for every amount claimed.
3. Programme, agenda, or attendance certificate showing the professional content and that the taxpayer took part.
4. Employer confirmation that neither costs nor time were reimbursed, or a signed self-declaration where the employer will not issue one in time.

A free event is not a problem in itself, provided the claimed costs are travel and accommodation only. It becomes a problem when the letter implies fees were paid. State it.

## Häusliches Arbeitszimmer und Tagespauschale

`§ 4 Abs. 5 S. 1 Nr. 6b EStG`, applicable to employees through `§ 9 Abs. 5 EStG`:

- Where the room is the **centre of the entire professional activity**: actual costs, or a `Jahrespauschale` without proof of amount.
- Otherwise: the `Tagespauschale` under `§ 4 Abs. 5 S. 1 Nr. 6c EStG` per day worked predominantly at home, capped per year.
- Until VZ 2022 a third route existed — a capped deduction where no other workplace was available. It was abolished from VZ 2023. Use the rule of the year under assessment; the amounts and the cut-over are in [`kennzahlen.md`](kennzahlen.md).

Requirements for the room itself: separate, closed room, furnished as an office, private use no more than insignificant. A work corner in the living room qualifies for neither route.

The evidence a `Belegaufforderung` asks for: floor plan with the room marked, areas of the room and of the flat, description of the activity performed there, statement that no other workplace was available where the year requires it, and the cost computation. Where an area is derived arithmetically rather than measured, say so in the document — a number that quietly claims to be measured collapses when the plan turns out to be undimensioned.

## Doppelte Haushaltsführung

`§ 9 Abs. 1 S. 3 Nr. 5 EStG` requires three elements: an **own household** at the centre of life, financial participation in its costs of more than 10 %, and a second dwelling at the place of work occupied for professional reasons. Living in a parent's household without bearing its costs defeats the first element — check it before offering double housekeeping as a fallback for a failed centre-of-life argument.

Deductible: accommodation cost up to a monthly cap, one family journey home per week at the commuting rate, moving costs, and meal allowances for the first three months. The monthly cap and the treatment of foreign accommodation are year-dependent; see [`kennzahlen.md`](kennzahlen.md).

## Arbeitsmittel

Items used predominantly for work. Below the `GWG` threshold of `§ 6 Abs. 2 EStG` — net of VAT — the full amount is deducted in the year of payment; above it, depreciated over the useful life from the AfA tables.

Computers, notebooks, peripherals, and operating and application software may be written off over a one-year useful life following the BMF letter of 22 February 2022, which in practice makes them fully deductible in the year of acquisition irrespective of price.

Telephone and internet: either evidenced professional share, or 20 % of the invoice up to a monthly ceiling under the wage-tax guidelines, which the tax offices generally accept for employees without further proof.

Watch two traps. A device claimed in one year must not appear again in another — check byte-identical invoices across year folders before submitting, and correct the other year under `§ 153 AO` where it happened. And where an item was bought late in the year, the year of **payment** governs, not the invoice date.

## Sonderausgaben

| Item | Norm | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Altersvorsorgeaufwendungen | § 10 Abs. 1 Nr. 2, Abs. 3 EStG | Fully deductible up to the cap since VZ 2023 |
| Kranken- und Pflegeversicherung, Basisabsicherung | § 10 Abs. 1 Nr. 3 EStG | Unlimited; supplementary cover only within the residual cap |
| Sonstige Vorsorgeaufwendungen | § 10 Abs. 1 Nr. 3a EStG | Usually exhausted by the basic health cover |
| Kirchensteuer | § 10 Abs. 1 Nr. 4 EStG | Paid less refunded |
| Kinderbetreuung | § 10 Abs. 1 Nr. 5 EStG | From VZ 2025: 80 % of the cost, max 4.800 € per child; until VZ 2024: two thirds, max 4.000 €. Invoice and cashless payment required |
| Berufsausbildung (Erstausbildung) | § 10 Abs. 1 Nr. 7 EStG | Capped, no loss carry-forward |
| Unterhalt an geschiedene Ehegatten | § 10 Abs. 1a Nr. 1 EStG | Realsplitting, needs the recipient's consent (Anlage U) |
| Spenden und Mitgliedsbeiträge | § 10b EStG | Up to 20 % of total income; `Zuwendungsbestätigung`, simplified proof for small amounts |

Health and pension contributions arrive at the tax office through `§ 10 Abs. 2b EStG` data transmission, so a deviation between the return and the assessment is usually the transmitted record — request the record before disputing the figure.

## Außergewöhnliche Belastungen

`§ 33 EStG`: unavoidable, larger than for the majority of comparable taxpayers, and reduced by the `zumutbare Belastung`. That reduction is computed in **three brackets** applied successively to the parts of total income (BFH VI R 75/14), not by applying one percentage to the whole — a common source of miscalculation in self-prepared returns.

Medical expenses require proof of medical necessity, and for the items in `§ 64 EStDV` — bath cures, psychotherapy, aids of general use — that proof must exist **before** the expense, as a prescription or an official medical opinion. Obtained afterwards it is worthless.

`§ 33a Abs. 1 EStG` covers maintenance to a legally supported person, capped at the basic allowance plus assumed basic insurance, reduced by the recipient's own income above a threshold; from VZ 2025 the payment must be by bank transfer. `§ 33b EStG` grants disability and care flat amounts on presentation of the relevant certificate.

## Haushaltsnahe Leistungen (§ 35a EStG)

A deduction **from the tax**, not from income:

| Category | Rate and ceiling | Norm |
|---|---|---|
| Geringfügige Beschäftigung im Haushalt | 20 %, max 510 euro | § 35a Abs. 1 |
| Haushaltsnahe Dienstleistungen, Pflege | 20 %, max 4,000 euro | § 35a Abs. 2 |
| Handwerkerleistungen | 20 %, max 1,200 euro | § 35a Abs. 3 |

Labour, travel, and machine cost only — never material. Two formal conditions defeat most claims: an **invoice** must exist, and payment must have been **cashless** (`§ 35a Abs. 5 S. 3 EStG`). Cash payment is fatal even with a receipt. Tenants may claim their share from the operating-cost settlement where it is itemised.

## Kapitalerträge

Private capital income is taxed at 25 % plus solidarity surcharge and church tax (`§ 32d Abs. 1 EStG`); actual expenses are barred, only the `Sparer-Pauschbetrag` applies (`§ 20 Abs. 9 EStG`). File `Anlage KAP` where tax was withheld incorrectly, where the allowance was not fully used, where church tax was not deducted, or where foreign income arrived without German withholding.

`Günstigerprüfung` (`§ 32d Abs. 6 EStG`) applies the personal rate where it is lower — always worth requesting for low total income, and it cannot make the result worse.

Foreign dividends: credit the foreign withholding tax under `§ 32d Abs. 5 EStG`, limited to the rate the double taxation treaty allows to the source state, typically 15 %. Anything withheld above that is recoverable only from the foreign authority. Convert to euro at the rate applicable at inflow and show the conversion, because the tax office reconciles against its own record.

Losses are ring-fenced by `§ 20 Abs. 6 EStG`: losses from shares only offset gains from shares. A loss certificate from the bank (`Verlustbescheinigung`) must be requested by 15 December of the year to move losses out of the bank's own pot.
