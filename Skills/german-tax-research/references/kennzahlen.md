## Tarif und Pauschbeträge

| VZ | Grundfreibetrag (§ 32a) | Arbeitnehmer-Pauschbetrag (§ 9a) | Sparer-Pauschbetrag, einzeln / zusammen (§ 20 Abs. 9) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2021 | 9.744 | 1.000 | 801 / 1.602 |
| 2022 | 10.347 | 1.200 | 801 / 1.602 |
| 2023 | 10.908 | 1.230 | 1.000 / 2.000 |
| 2024 | 11.784 | 1.230 | 1.000 / 2.000 |
| 2025 | 12.096 | 1.230 | 1.000 / 2.000 |
| 2026 | 12.348 | 1.230 | 1.000 / 2.000 |

The 2022 basic allowance and employee flat amount were raised retroactively by the `Steuerentlastungsgesetz 2022`; the 2024 allowance was raised retroactively by the `Gesetz zur steuerlichen Freistellung des Existenzminimums 2024`. Assessments issued before those acts carry the lower figures.

`Sonderausgaben-Pauschbetrag` (`§ 10c EStG`): 36 euro, 72 euro on joint assessment, unchanged across all years above.

From VZ 2026 the employee flat amount no longer absorbs union dues — they are deducted in addition (`§ 9a S. 3 EStG`, StÄndG 2025).

## Arbeitszimmer und Homeoffice

| VZ | Room is the centre of the activity | No other workplace available | Working from home without a qualifying room |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2021–2022 | Actual costs, unlimited | Actual costs up to 1.250 euro per year | 5 euro per day, max 600 euro (120 days) |
| from 2023 | Actual costs, or `Jahrespauschale` 1.260 euro | Route abolished | `Tagespauschale` 6 euro per day, max 1.260 euro (210 days) |

Norms: `§ 4 Abs. 5 S. 1 Nr. 6b EStG` for the room, `Nr. 6c` for the daily allowance, applied to employees through `§ 9 Abs. 5 EStG`. The 1,250 euro ceiling of the older years is a **Höchstbetrag**, so a computed share above it is capped rather than rejected.

## Fahrten, Reisen, doppelte Haushaltsführung

| VZ | Entfernungspauschale (§ 9 Abs. 1 S. 3 Nr. 4) | Verpflegung 24 h / An- und Abreise or > 8 h (§ 9 Abs. 4a) | Unterkunft doppelte Haushaltsführung (§ 9 Abs. 1 S. 3 Nr. 5) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2021 | 0,30 € to km 20; 0,35 € from km 21 | 28 € / 14 € | 1.000 € per month |
| 2022–2025 | 0,30 € to km 20; 0,38 € from km 21 | 28 € / 14 € | 1.000 € per month |
| 2026 | 0,38 € from km 1 | 28 € / 14 € | 1.000 € Inland, 2.000 € Ausland per month |

The 0.38 euro rate for kilometres from 21 applies retroactively from 1 January 2022 (`Steuerentlastungsgesetz 2022`); the flat rate from the first kilometre and the foreign accommodation ceiling come from the `Steueränderungsgesetz 2025` with effect from 1 January 2026.

Ceiling of 4,500 euro per year unless the taxpayer used their own or a company car (`§ 9 Abs. 1 S. 3 Nr. 4 S. 2 EStG`). Own car for a business trip: 0.30 euro per kilometre **driven** (`§ 9 Abs. 1 S. 3 Nr. 4a S. 2 EStG`). Meal allowance reductions where meals were provided: 20 % for breakfast, 40 % each for lunch and dinner, of the 24-hour rate. Foreign per-diems: annual BMF letter, cited by date.

## Zumutbare Belastung (§ 33 Abs. 3 EStG)

Applied in three brackets to the parts of the `Gesamtbetrag der Einkünfte`, not as one percentage on the whole (BFH VI R 75/14):

| Situation | up to 15.340 € | up to 51.130 € | above 51.130 € |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Single, no children | 5 % | 6 % | 7 % |
| Joint assessment, no children | 4 % | 5 % | 6 % |
| One or two children | 2 % | 3 % | 4 % |
| Three or more children | 1 % | 1 % | 2 % |

## Abgabefristen der Steuererklärung

`§ 149 AO`, extended for the assessment periods 2020 to 2024 by `§ 36 EGAO`. Dates already shifted for weekends under `§ 108 Abs. 3 AO`.

| VZ | Without tax adviser | With tax adviser |
|---|---|---|
| 2021 | 31.10.2022 * | 31.08.2023 |
| 2022 | 02.10.2023 | 31.07.2024 |
| 2023 | 02.09.2024 | 02.06.2025 |
| 2024 | 31.07.2025 | 30.04.2026 |
| 2025 | 31.07.2026 | 01.03.2027 |
| 2026 onward | 31.07. of the following year | last day of February of the second following year |

\* 31 October 2022 is `Reformationstag` in several federal states; there the deadline moved to 1 November 2022. Public holidays are state law, so check the state of the competent Finanzamt.

For voluntary filing (`Antragsveranlagung`, `§ 46 Abs. 2 Nr. 8 EStG`) the limit is four years from the end of the assessment period, and the extensions above do not apply.
