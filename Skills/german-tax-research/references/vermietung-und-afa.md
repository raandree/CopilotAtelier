## Einnahmen

Gross rent plus all `Umlagen` and advance payments received, plus withheld deposits once forfeited, plus subsidies that are not repayable. `§ 11 Abs. 1 EStG` governs timing: what was received in the year, with the ten-day rule for regularly recurring items around the turn of the year.

Report **gross**. A bank credit is often a net figure: the manager may have deducted their fee, or paid a former tenant's operating-cost credit out of the incoming rent. Both cases produce income at the gross amount and a separate expense — reconstruct from the manager's statement, not from the bank line.

Operating-cost settlements are income in the year of the credit received and expense in the year of the repayment made, irrespective of the year they relate to.

## Werbungskosten bei V+V

`§ 9 Abs. 1 EStG` in the form applying to `§ 21 EStG`. The usual catalogue:

- `AfA` on the building share (never on land).
- `Schuldzinsen` on loans economically tied to the property; repayment instalments are not deductible.
- `Erhaltungsaufwand`, `Hausgeld` less the reserve allocation, `Grundsteuer`, insurance, `Verwaltergebühren`.
- `Vermietungsprovision`, advertising, viewing costs; travel to the property at the flat rate, with a log of purpose and date.
- Legal and tax advice attributable to the letting.
- Operating costs not passed on, and those passed on where the corresponding income is reported.

Payments to the `Erhaltungsrücklage` of a `Wohnungseigentümergemeinschaft` are not deductible when paid; they become deductible when the reserve is spent on maintenance (BFH IX R 19/24 confirms the long-standing rule after the WEG reform).

## Erhaltungsaufwand vs. Herstellungskosten

| Type | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Erhaltungsaufwand | Fully deductible in the year of payment |
| Größerer Erhaltungsaufwand | May be spread over two to five years (`§ 82b EStDV`), by election, for buildings not part of business assets |
| Herstellungskosten | Capitalised, deducted through AfA |
| Anschaffungsnaher Herstellungsaufwand | Capitalised, see below |

`§ 6 Abs. 1 Nr. 1a EStG`: renovation expenses within three years of acquisition that exceed 15 % of the acquisition cost of the building — net of VAT — count as `Herstellungskosten` and can only be depreciated. Annually recurring maintenance and expenses for extension are excluded from the calculation. The three-year period runs from acquisition, and the threshold is tested on the total across those years, so a second-year invoice can retroactively convert first-year deductions.

## AfA-Sätze für Gebäude

`§ 7 Abs. 4 S. 1 EStG`, on the building share of the acquisition or production cost:

| Building | Rate | Norm |
|---|---|---|
| Business asset, not residential, Bauantrag after 31 March 1985 | 3 % | § 7 Abs. 4 S. 1 Nr. 1 |
| Otherwise, completed after 31 December 2022 | 3 % | § 7 Abs. 4 S. 1 Nr. 2 a |
| Otherwise, completed after 31 December 1924 and before 1 January 2023 | 2 % | § 7 Abs. 4 S. 1 Nr. 2 b |
| Otherwise, completed before 1 January 1925 | 2.5 % | § 7 Abs. 4 S. 1 Nr. 2 c |

A shorter actual useful life may be used where it is below 33, 50, or 40 years respectively (`§ 7 Abs. 4 S. 2 EStG`) — in practice this needs a qualified opinion, and `§ 11c Abs. 1 EStDV` defines the concept.

In the year of acquisition or production, AfA is reduced by one twelfth for each full month before the month of acquisition (`§ 7 Abs. 1 S. 4 EStG`, applicable via `§ 7 Abs. 4 S. 1 Hs. 2`).

Historic accelerated schedules under `§ 7 Abs. 5 EStG` still run for older buildings; where an inherited AfA series exists, continue it rather than restarting from a current rate.

## Degressive AfA für Wohngebäude (§ 7 Abs. 5a EStG)

5 % of the declining book value per year, for residential buildings in the EU or EEA where construction began after 30 September 2023 and before 1 October 2029, or where acquisition rests on a contract concluded in that window. Construction start is the date on the `Baubeginnsanzeige`. A switch to straight-line AfA is permitted at any time and is then computed on the remaining value and remaining useful life (`§ 7 Abs. 5a S. 7, 8 EStG`); a switch in the other direction is not.

Exceptional depreciation for technical or economic wear is not available while the declining-balance method is used.

## Sonderabschreibung Mietwohnungsneubau (§ 7b EStG)

Up to 5 % per year for four years **in addition to** the AfA under `§ 7 Abs. 4` or `Abs. 5a`, for new rental dwellings within the statutory application windows, subject to cost caps per square metre and a ten-year letting requirement. Check the current version of the norm for the applicable windows and caps before claiming it — they have been amended repeatedly.

## Sanierungsgebiet und Baudenkmal (§§ 7h, 7i EStG)

For qualifying production costs: up to 9 % per year in the year of production and the following seven years, then up to 7 % for four years — twelve years in total.

The decisive procedural point: the certificate from the competent authority (`§ 7h Abs. 2`, `§ 7i Abs. 2 EStG`) is a **Grundlagenbescheid**. Without it the elevated depreciation is not assessable, and with it the assessment must be amended under `§ 175 Abs. 1 S. 1 Nr. 1 AO` however final it had become. Certification procedures routinely take years.

Practical handling while the certificate is outstanding:

1. Claim the ordinary AfA on the underlying acquisition cost, so the basic deduction is secured.
2. State the elevated amount separately and expressly as an unattested application figure, naming the application date, the file number, and the authority.
3. Ask for provisional assessment (`§ 165 Abs. 1 S. 1 AO`) or for the objection to rest (`§ 363 Abs. 2 S. 1 AO`) until the certificate issues.
4. Never merge the attested and unattested amounts into a single figure — that turns an open procedural point into an unprovable assertion.

## Kaufpreisaufteilung

Depreciation runs on the building share only. Order of preference:

1. A split agreed in the notarial contract, provided it is economically reasonable and not a mere formula; the courts respect it as a starting point.
2. Otherwise, a split by relative fair values of land and building.
3. The BMF `Arbeitshilfe zur Kaufpreisaufteilung` is **not binding**. BFH IX R 26/19 held it may not be used as the sole basis in dispute; where the parties disagree, an expert opinion decides.

Document the derivation in the return itself. A split asserted without a derivation is the single most frequently challenged figure in `Anlage V`.

## Verbilligte Überlassung (§ 21 Abs. 2 EStG)

| Rent as % of local market rent | Consequence |
|---|---|
| ≥ 66 % | Full deduction of expenses, no forecast required |
| 50 % to under 66 % | Full deduction only if a positive total-surplus forecast succeeds; otherwise apportionment |
| < 50 % | Mandatory split into a paid and an unpaid part; expenses deductible only pro rata |

The comparison is against the local rent including operating costs (`ortsübliche Warmmiete`). Document the comparison basis — rent index, comparable flats — in the year the arrangement starts.

## Überschusserzielungsabsicht

For long-term letting of residential property, the intent to generate a surplus is presumed. The presumption breaks on unusual arrangements: extended vacancy without letting efforts, holiday lets used personally, loss-making structures with visibly no path to a surplus. Where the Finanzamt doubts it, expect an assessment declared provisional under `§ 165 Abs. 1 S. 1 AO` on exactly that point, sometimes carried forward across several years. Track which years carry that reservation, because the provisionality is the route through which they are all later reopened.

## Untervermietung und gemischt genutzte Wohnungen

Subletting part of a rented flat produces `Einkünfte aus Vermietung und Verpachtung` (`§ 21 Abs. 1 S. 1 Nr. 1 EStG`). Compute it explicitly and keep the arithmetic in one place:

1. Total costs of the flat for the year, itemised by cost type with an evidence column.
2. Total floor area and the area let, from a plan or a measurement, not from memory.
3. Cost per square metre, then the share attributable to the sublet area.
4. Sublet income received, less the attributable share, equals the result.

Where the Finanzamt applied a particular key in an earlier year, reuse that key and quote the earlier notice verbatim; a self-improved key invites a reassessment of the whole computation. Label any area that was reconciled arithmetically — a corridor derived as the balance to a contractual total — as derived rather than measured.

## Beleglage je Objekt

Keep these ready per property; they are what a `Belegaufforderung` for `Anlage V` asks for:

- Purchase contract and the derivation of the building share.
- AfA schedule since acquisition, with the annual amount, the rate, and the norm.
- Loan agreements and annual interest statements.
- Lease agreements and the annual rent account.
- Operating-cost settlements and the manager's annual statement.
- Invoices for maintenance, with payment evidence.
- For `§ 7h`/`§ 7i`: application, receipt confirmation, correspondence, certificate once issued.
